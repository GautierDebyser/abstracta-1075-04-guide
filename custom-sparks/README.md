The user confirmed that labeled inch lengths take priority over repeated codes.
530 C is used for both 18.00″ and 18.50″; 675 C for 24.00″ and 24.50″.
Keep these as separate inventory items. The C suffix is preserved, not assumed
to denote curvature. No curved tubes are shown in the custom drawing.

The two inner uprights have staggered side-bay and center-bay connections.
They do not share a single regular grid of shelf heights. Nominal ½″ joint
allowances reconcile all cut-length chains with the printed overall dimensions.
The rendered 13 mm tube diameter is slightly different from nominal ½″.
Connector insertions, castings, wall thickness and tolerances are unmeasured.
All casting meshes are explicitly schematic. No manufacturing accuracy is claimed.

The guide proposes a vertical-ladder assembly sequence, with the rear frame
staged apart until step 9, based on the manufacturer's general ladder method.
The upright view is explanatory: support the frames during physical assembly.
Stability, anchoring and safe loading require supplier confirmation.
