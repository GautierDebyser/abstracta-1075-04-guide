Facilitator instructions

Test the guide, not the participant. Invite someone unfamiliar with Abstracta to think aloud. Present one task at a time without coaching, pointing to controls or correcting interpretations. Record the first action, exact questions, hesitation and mistakes. Ask “What would you do next?” Record any later assistance separately. Do not request physical lifting, hammering or loading during this comprehension review. Suggested duration: 20–30 minutes.
